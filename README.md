# Zen 6 Coder — retired

Zen 6 Coder is retired, and its weights are no longer published.

The Zen 6 generation is [Zen 6](https://github.com/zenlm/zen6) and
[Zen 6 Flash](https://github.com/zenlm/zen6-flash), from Zen LM, the open model
family of [Zoo Labs Foundation](https://zoo.ngo), a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Both
answer on `api.hanzo.ai` as `zen6` and `zen6-flash`. Zen 7 is in research
preview: [request access](https://hanzo.ai/research-access).

`training/` keeps the post-training recipe this repository carried.
